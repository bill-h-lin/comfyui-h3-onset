What this is

One impact travelling through a chain of materials. I wrote the sound before the pictures existed, and every beat I placed came back where I put it. H3 gives you no handle on when it makes a sound — but a transient written into the bound reference comes back in the render at the time you put it. So the rhythm is authored first and the model performs it.

What I am not claiming: not every sound here was written. On the glass, ceramic and melon shots the loudest thing in the shot is the model's own — a depicted break makes H3 generate an event at the head of the render, unbidden, scaling with how violent the picture line is. Those are real, synced and free, but I did not command them. The beats I did command are a separate, measured set.

Picture and audio come from the same H3 pass in every shot. Nothing was overlaid, recorded, or attached afterwards; the joins carry room tone from the two shots being spliced.

The one thing worth stealing

Seven transients placed at 1000/1900/2548/3015/3350/3592/3766 ms came back at +27.0 … +12.2 dB over an otherwise byte-identical carrier, 3 of 3 seeds on every one; seven decoy times with nothing placed read −0.3 … +1.4 dB, also 3/3. Gaps down to 174 ms still resolve — but the real limit is position, not gap: in a 124-frame render everything commanded at or before ~3.02 s reproduces and everything at or after ~3.35 s does not, deterministic across 3 seeds × 2 subjects. A 243 ms gap fires at 3.00 s and a 242 ms gap fails at 3.59 s. Put your beats in the first three seconds. Several deltas exceed the cue's own amplitude — the model generates a fuller event than you wrote in, so the reference only says when.

What it does not do: it does not command the picture. A pre-registered test of picture-follows-sound failed here. Visible agreement is something you select for across takes and then land with the cut. (The ~57% selection rate I measured is from an earlier subject class, not this film — I have not re-measured it here, so treat it as an order of magnitude, not a number for these shots.) I'd rather say that than imply the model choreographed the image.

I would rather you find that here than in the JSON.

AI generation disclosure. Every shot in this film — picture and audio together — was generated by MiniMax H3 (reference-to-video) in ComfyUI v0.32.0, running locally on one RTX PRO 6000. No sound was recorded, licensed or overlaid; the audio in each shot came out of the same H3 pass as its picture. Editing was limited to cutting, level ramps at head and tail, and 120 ms of room tone taken from the two shots being joined. No people appear in the film.
